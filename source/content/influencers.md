## The program

25 creators in four groups get early access about three weeks before launch, post in three waves, and show up on Product Hunt on launch day with their own honest take, never a vote request.

### Who

| Group | Count | Who they are | Main channel | Their job |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Product Hunt regulars | 4 | Active makers and community leaders in dev tools and productivity | Product Hunt, X | First genuine comments at launch |
| Docs-as-code and DevRel voices | 9 | Developer advocates, docs engineers, open-source maintainers who keep docs in repos | X, YouTube, LinkedIn | Show a real repo syncing both ways, and a PR merged from the editor |
| Technical writing voices | 6 | Writers, docs leads, Write the Docs speakers | LinkedIn, newsletters, blogs | Speak to editing without Git and resolving conflicts in plain language |
| Product and SaaS leaders | 6 | PMs and founders with B2B audiences | LinkedIn | Speak to one source of truth and release velocity |

### When

| Wave | Timing | What they post | Link |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Onboarding | 3 weeks out | Nothing yet: a 30-minute demo, early access, the content kit | — |
| Wave 1: first look | 2 weeks out | A clip of Lotus syncing their own repo, plus "follow Document360 on Product Hunt" | Waitlist (personal UTM) |
| Wave 2: next week | 1 week out | A short "launching next week" post | Document360 Product Hunt page |
| Launch day | Assigned slot | Their honest take, then their own comment on the launch | Product Hunt launch |
| Wave 3: deep dive | Week after | A tutorial: moving a docs repo into Lotus, or a docs-as-code workflow for writers | Landing page (personal UTM) |

**Launch-day slots follow the sun:** India 1:30 – 4:00 PM IST, Europe 9:00 – 11:00 AM CET, US East 9:00 – 11:00 AM ET, US West 9:00 – 11:00 AM PST.

### What they get

Early access, a 30-minute demo, and a content kit: the 15-second clip, the hero visual and mascot, three GIFs (commit → article, edit → pull request, conflict resolved), a sample repo with `d360.json`, the coding-agent prompt, three key messages and the reply bank.

### Brief

- **Do:** show Lotus on a real repo; show both directions of sync; show a conflict being resolved; say you're partnering with Document360 (#ad or "in partnership with"); write in your own voice; use your personal link.
- **Don't:** ask anyone to upvote; claim GitLab or Bitbucket support; call it "the only" two-way sync; share the Product Hunt link before launch day.

### Two starter posts creators can adapt

> I connected one of my docs repos to @Document360's new Lotus. Committed in GitHub: the article updated. Edited in the browser: it opened a pull request. Then I forced a conflict, and it showed both versions side by side with "keep both." Launching on Product Hunt soon. Early access: [personal link] (in partnership with Document360)

> Docs people: I just merged a docs pull request without opening a terminal. Lotus keeps a GitHub repo and Document360 in sync both ways, so writers edit in an editor and developers keep their PRs. Launching on Product Hunt soon. #ad

### Tracking

One sheet with each creator's group, wave status, post links, clicks and waitlist signups (`utm_source=influencer`, `utm_content=<their name>`), and whether they commented on launch day. Review it every Friday.
