## The job before launch day

Turn a quiet product page into a launch-ready one: refresh it, grow it from 19 to 1,000+ followers, and line up 500+ people who will show up on the day.

Today the [Document360 page](https://www.producthunt.com/products/document360) shows 19 followers, 2 reviews and 3 launches, the last in 2021. [Mintlify's page](https://www.producthunt.com/products/mintlify/launches/mintlify-autopilot) has 2.4K followers. Followers hear about a new launch first, and Product Hunt has retired its old "Notify me" pages, so following the product page is now the main way to be told.

### 2. Grow followers from 19 to 1,000+

Every pre-launch touchpoint ends with one ask: **follow Document360 on Product Hunt to hear when Lotus launches.** Add it to both newsletters, the portal notifications, the community post, team email signatures, influencer waves 1 and 2, and the reminder email.

### 3. Build a supporter list of 500+

One shared sheet with name, region (India/APAC, Europe, US), Product Hunt username, channel and launch-day wave. Start with current GitHub extension users; they have the most to gain and the most to say.

### 4. Warm up the Product Hunt forum

Three threads on [p/document360](https://www.producthunt.com/p/document360):

1. "Where do your docs live: in the repo, in a knowledge base, or both?" (poll)
2. "What's your worst docs merge conflict story?"
3. "Lotus launches next week. What should two-way GitHub sync handle on day one?"

### 5. Activate team accounts now

8 to 10 team members log in daily and comment thoughtfully on launches in knowledge base and developer tools. Product Hunt weights votes and comments from established, active accounts more than new ones.

### 6. Teasers on our own channels

| When | Post | Link to |

**Rules for everyone:** never ask for upvotes. Ask people to check out the launch and share honest feedback. No vote groups, no shared accounts, and influencers disclose that they're working with us.
